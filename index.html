The Liquid Hotplates

UC Davis's HOTTEST acapella group.

Who We Are

Established in 2000, The Liquid Hotplates is University of California, Davis's hottest all-gender a cappella group. Every year, we compete at ICCAs (International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella) & most recently placed 3rd at ICCA West Semifinals in which we recieved awards for Outstanding Vocal Percussion & Outstanding Soloists. We also host two of our own showcases - Alottacappella in the fall, and Off The Record in the spring!
