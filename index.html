Who We Are

Established in 2000, The Liquid Hotplates is University of California, Davis's hottest all-gender a cappella group on campus. Every year, we compete in the ICCAs (International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella) and host two of our own showcases — Alottacappella in the fall and Off The Record in the spring. Whether we're on a competition stage or our home turf, we bring the same energy: tight harmonies, bold arrangements, and a whole lot of heat.
