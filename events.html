Events

The Liquid Hotplates hosts two annual showcases - Alottacappella & Off the Record every year - one in the fall and one in late spring. Additionally, LHP also attends the International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella Competition (ICCAs) hosted by Varsity Vocals every year in February.

More information along with pictures and details about each of these events can be found by clicking on the events below!
