ICCA West Quarterfinals 2025

February 8th, 2025 • Fox Theatre @ Redwood City

About ICCA West Quarterfinals 2025

3rd Place - Quarterfinals Outstanding Vocal Percussion

LHP competed at the ICCA West Quarterfinals and took home 3rd place (AGAIN!).Lik Yau Chow was awarded Outstanding Vocal Percussion.

We had 6 new babyplates perform at their first ICCA this round!

Setlist

  1. "West Coast" - OneRepublic
    • Soloists: Brooklyn Mercado, Noah Sifry
    • Arrangement: Evan Liu
    • Choreographer: Kiera Yap
  2. "Everything Matters" - AURORA
    • Soloist: Kiera Yap
    • End trio: Rounak Dey, Isabella Hurst, Ellie Mizushima
    • Arrangement: Chow Lik Yau
    • Choreographer: Ellie Mizushima
  3. "Fall In Line" - Christina Aguilera Feat. Demi Lovato
    • Soloists: Ellie Mizushima, Alyza Gomez
    • Arrangement: Chow Lik Yau, Evan Liu
    • Choreographer: Ellie Mizushima
