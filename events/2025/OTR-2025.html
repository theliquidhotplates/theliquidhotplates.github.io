Off The Record 2025

May 17th, 2025 • California Hall, UC Davis

About Off The Record 2025

Off The Record was LHP's first show of its kind - a free LHP-focused spring show focused on showcasing all of the music we worked on the entire year, small groups & solos, along with member-made merch available for purchase.

We also said goodbye to two of our beloved Class of 2025 seniors, Rounak Day & Noah Sifry.

Setlist

  1. "Worth It" - RAYE
  2. "Crazy/Take Me to Church (Mashup)" - Gnarls Barkley & Hozier
  3. "Wildflower" - Billie Eilish
  4. "Tum Hi Ho" - Arijit Singh
  5. 2025 ICCA Set
