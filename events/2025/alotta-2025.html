About Alottacappella

Alottacappella is LHP's annual showcase hosted in the Fall in which we invite groups from around California to come and perform at our show! This is a two day event in which different groups are present on different days.

This year the Saturday theme was Game Day, and the Sunday theme was Casino Night!

Setlist

  1. "Your Idol" - Kpop Demon Hunters
  2. "Crazy" - Gnarls Barkley
  3. "Attention" - Charlie Puth
  4. "Take Me to Church" - Hozier
  5. "Ashes and Blood" - Arcane
  6. "Yebba Mashup" - Yebba
