About Alottacappella

Alottacappella is LHP's annual showcase hosted in the Fall in which we invite groups from around California to come and perform at our show! This is a two day event in which different groups are present on different days. This year the Saturday theme was Game Day, and the Sunday theme was Casino Night!

Setlist

  1. "Can't Help Falling in Love" - Elvis Presley
  2. "Levitating" - Dua Lipa
  3. "Shallow" - Lady Gaga & Bradley Cooper
  4. "Mr. Brightside" - The Killers
  5. "Someone Like You" - Adele
  6. "Don't Stop Believin'" - Journey
