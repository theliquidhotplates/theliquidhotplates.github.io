Alottacappella 2024

November 16th-17th, 2024 • California Hall, UC Davis

About Alottacappella 2024

Alottacappella is LHP's annual showcase hosted in the Fall in which we invite groups from around California to come and perform at our show! This is a two day event in which different groups are present on different days.

This year, the theme on Saturday was Adam Sandler, and Sunday's theme was Black & White. Guest groups wre encouraged to also participate in the theme.

Setlist

  1. "Double Life" - Pharrell Williams
  2. "West Coast" - OneRepublic
  3. "Hrs & Hrs" - Muni Long
  4. "Pray" - Sam Smith
  5. "Everything Matters" - Aurora
  6. "Yebba Mashup" (Alumni Song) - Yebba
