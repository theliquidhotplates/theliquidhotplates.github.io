About ICCA West Quarterfinals 2024

3rd Place - Quarterfinals Outstanding Soloist

LHP competed at the 2024 ICCA West Quarterfinals and took home 3rd. Alyza Gomez was also awarded Outstanding Soloist!

  4. "Genius" - Sia, Diplo and Labrinth
