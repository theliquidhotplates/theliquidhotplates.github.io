February 28th, 2026 • Fox Theatre @ Redwood City

About ICCA West Quarterfinals 2026

1st Place - Quarterfinals Best Vocal Percussion Outstanding Soloist Outstanding Arrangement

LHP took home 1st place at the ICCA West Quarterfinals after performing our ICCA Competition set for the first time! Lik Yau Chow won Outstanding Vocal Percussion AND Outstanding Arrangement, and Evan Liu was awarded Outstanding Soloist.

This is the first time in history that LHP has placed so high at Quarterfinals!

Setlist

    • Soloist: Priyanshi Singh
    • Arrangement: Chow Lik Yau

  2. "To Ashes & Blood" - Arcane, League of Legends, and Woodkid
    • Soloist: Evan Liu
    • Arrangement: Chow Lik Yau
    • Choreographers: Ellie Mizushima, Kiera Yap
  3. "这样很好[Isha's Song]/First Light" - Eason Chan, League of Legends, and Hozier
    • Soloists: Lesley Laurence, Ellie Mizushima
