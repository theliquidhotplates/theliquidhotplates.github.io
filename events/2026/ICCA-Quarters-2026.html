About ICCA West Quarterfinals 2026

LHP took 1st place at the ICCA West Quarterfinals, one of the qualifying rounds of the International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella (ICCA) - one of the biggest collegiate a cappella competition circuits in the country. Lik Yau Chow was awarded Best Vocal Percussion, and Evan Liu was awarded Outstanding Soloist.
