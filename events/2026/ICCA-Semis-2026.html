ICCA West Semifinals 2026

March 21st, 2026 • Fox Theatre @ Redwood City

About ICCA West Semifinals 2026

3rd Place - Semifinals Outstanding Vocal Percussion Outstanding Soloist

After winning 1st place at Quarterfinals, LHP advanced to the ICCA West Semifinals, competing against some of the top groups from across the West region. We took home 3rd place along with Outstanding Vocal Percussion (won by Lik Yau Chow) and Outstanding Soloist (won by Lesley Laurence)!

Setlist

  1. The Blade - Aurora
  2. Ashes & Blood - Arcane, League of Legends, and Woodkid
  3. Isha's/First Light (Mashup) - Eason Chan, League of Legends, and Hozier
