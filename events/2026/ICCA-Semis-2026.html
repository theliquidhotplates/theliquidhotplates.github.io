About ICCA West Semifinals 2026

3rd Place - Semifinals Outstanding Vocal Percussion Outstanding Soloist

After winning 1st place at Quarterfinals, LHP moved on to the ICCA West Semifinals, competing against some of the top groups from across the West region. We took home 3rd place along with Outstanding Vocal Percussion (won by Lik Yau Chow) and Outstanding Soloist (won by Lesley Laurence)!

We also got amazing shots of our set by Jake Young (pictured below)!
