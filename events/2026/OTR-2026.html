About Off The Record 2026

LHP presents our annual free spring showcase - Off The Record! This year, we performed various songs arranged by many members in the group, along with our award-winning ICCA set. We also said goodbye to our Class of 2026 Seniors - Ellie Mizushima & Priyanshi Singh!
